The source FBX supplied for this mod has been converted to a self-contained runtime mesh/rig payload and uses procedural bone motion. No original textures were present in the uploaded FBX; the mod assigns a plain ceramic material and generates basic visor/weapon details.
Preserve the author attribution, source URL and license when redistributing the model.
